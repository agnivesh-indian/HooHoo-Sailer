📖 About
HooHoo Sailer is a slow-breathing biofeedback experience grounded in heart-rate variability (HRV) research. Slow paced breathing at ~6 breaths per minute increases vagal tone, reduces cortisol, and calms the nervous system.

About
HooHoo Sailer uses slow-paced breathing at ~6 breaths per minute, a protocol shown in HRV research to increase vagal tone, reduce cortisol, and support emotional regulation. The sea responds to your exhale — the boat stays anchored while the ocean surges forward with the power of your breath.
