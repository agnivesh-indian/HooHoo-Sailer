📖 About
HooHoo Sailer is a slow-breathing biofeedback experience grounded in heart-rate variability (HRV) research. Slow paced breathing at ~6 breaths per minute increases vagal tone, reduces cortisol, and calms the nervous system.
🎯 How to sail
1
Watch the ocean. When the sea calms and the badge shows Calm Seas, breathe in — the waves will build around you.
2
When waves rise high and the badge shows Ride the Waves, press and hold the button to exhale and harness their power.
!
Don't miss an exhale. If the storm surge overwhelms the boat, your ship sinks and the voyage restarts.
3
Advance through levels. Each level brings bigger waves and new seas — from Open Sea to the Great Unknown.
Every exhale counts. Miss one and the voyage restarts.
