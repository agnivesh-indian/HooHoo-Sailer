Breeze

Breathe in. Sail out.

📖 About
Breeze is a slow-breathing biofeedback game grounded in heart-rate variability (HRV) research. Breathing at roughly 6 breaths per minute (4s in, 6s out) has been shown to increase vagal tone, reduce cortisol, and shift the nervous system toward a calm, focused state. You sail by exhaling — the calmer and steadier your breath, the further you go.
🎯 How to play
1
Breathe in when the banner says Breathe in. Let your chest expand naturally for 4 seconds.
2
Hold the button when the banner says Breathe out — keep holding for the entire 6-second exhale.
3
Watch your boat sail. Steady, full exhales generate the most wind. Release too early and the sail goes flat.
4
Complete cycles to build coherence. Aim for a full sail at the end of each exhale.

About
Breeze uses slow-paced breathing at ~6 breaths per minute, a protocol shown in HRV research to increase vagal tone, reduce cortisol, and support emotional regulation. The game mechanic — exhale to fill the sail — encourages long, steady exhales, which are particularly effective for parasympathetic activation.
